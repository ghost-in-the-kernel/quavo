WHAT MAKES DRE DIFFERENT

A team of experts that applies their knowledge to your policies & procedures

In most dispute operations a claim arrives and becomes one person’s job to move, chase, and remember. In Quavo’s QFD platform, the system holds the claim instead. Its AI assembles the evidence, scores the claim against how similar disputes resolved, applies the rules that govern that payment type, and tracks what is owed and when, so nothing stalls because nobody got to it. DRE specialists are the heaviest users of that platform, which is why the same person works a case faster here than they could anywhere else.

Every decision draws on the whole network

Your own history shows what happened at your institution. QFD’s AI shows how claims like this one resolved across hundreds of others, including which recoveries held, and your specialist starts from there.

Network mandates arrive as product updates

Intake is built backward from what the networks require to file and what an investigator needs to close. When the April and October mandates change what you have to ask, the change ships in the platform instead of becoming a project on your roadmap.

Your policy, enforced by  the platform

Set provisional credit timing, denial standards, and escalation thresholds to how your institution reads the regulation. We hold every case to your ideal approach.

How it works

Expert judgment on the cases that need it,  all in one system of record

Quavo’s AI gathers the evidence and scores each claim, so our experts start every case with the full picture. They work it by your policies, in the same platform and audit trail your team relies on.

Your team takes the claim,
and the timers start on their own

Intake stays with your staff, your channels, and your first conversation with the accountholder. As soon as the claim is filed, QFD begins the regulatory timers for both Reg E and Reg Z claims. Automated actions run on the rules you configure, and nothing gets re-keyed into a vendor's system.

The evidence and the scores are attached before the case is assigned

Quavo's AI assembles the transaction detail and supporting evidence, then scores the claim on likely recapture, denial, chargeback win, and whether the recovery will hold. The recommendation and the reasoning behind it land on the case file.

Under your threshold the system acts, over it a specialist works the case

You set the thresholds. Clear calls are worked automatically on the recommended path. Everything else is routed alongside relevant scores and evidence to a DRE specialist who works the case inside your policies and documents the reasoning on the claim.

Every action logged, with the rule behind it

Cardholder correspondence goes out on time, the resolution posts, and each action is recorded with the rule that drove it. You review DRE's work the same way you review your own team's.

Deadlines are held by QFD, not by whoever is working the case

Every DRE case follows the regulatory and network requirements built into QFD, including Regulation E, Regulation Z, Nacha requirements, and card network rules. Timelines, correspondence, and documentation stay on track whether the work is automated or handled by a specialist, and every action is timestamped for examiners.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about DRE

How is DRE different from a BPO?
With a BPO, the work leaves your systems and comes back as a report. With DRE, every case stays in QFD as an end-to-end system of record where you can see who worked it and what they decided. Our experts apply your policies to every call and QFD logs the rule behind each one, so the outcome stays yours to own and defend.
What work does the DRE team handle?

DRE specialists handle case work before and after the chargeback, including provisional credit issuance, the first investigation, representments, pre-arbitration filings, and arbitration escalations. A small amount of work stays with you, mainly manual accounting adjustments, accountholder inquiries, and exception tasks that require your internal systems.

Do we need to be a QFD customer first?

No. DRE works whether or not you run QFD today. Existing customers keep their instance, workflows, and case history, and their specialists work inside it. If you’re new to Quavo, the platform is part of how the service is delivered, so there is nothing for your team to implement before the work starts.

Does DRE handle claim intake?

Intake stays with your team, your channels, and your first conversation with the customer.

What data and reporting do we get?

Everything DRE does is documented on the case in QFD. You get dashboards, the reporting browser, data warehouse access, and business event streaming, plus recurring business reviews with DRE team leads covering casework quality, volume health, and recovery opportunities.

How flexible is the scope?

DRE scales up and down with your needs, across card, ATM, ACH, and non-card disputes, with expertise across Visa and Mastercard and deep Reg E and Reg Z knowledge. Take on a seasonal spike, a single recovery step, a full back-office handoff, or anything in between.
