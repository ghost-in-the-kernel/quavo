Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Fraud
& Dispute Management

What is the fraud management process?

Fraud management is the end-to-end practice of identifying, investigating, and resolving fraudulent transactions. Quavo’s QFD® automates every stage, from intake through resolution, reducing manual work and compliance risk.

What is the difference between fraud and dispute?

Fraud involves unauthorized transactions where the cardholder had no involvement. A dispute occurs when a cardholder challenges a charge, which may or may not involve fraud. QFD® handles both with purpose-built workflows.

What is the most successful reason for disputing a charge?

Unauthorized transactions, merchandise not received, and services not rendered are among the most commonly upheld disputes. Strong documentation and timely filing significantly improve outcomes.

What are the 4 Ps of fraud?
The 4 Ps (Predispose, Pressure, Perceive, and Perpetrate) describe the conditions that lead to fraudulent behavior, underscoring the value of proactive monitoring and deterrence.
What is the 10/80/10 rule for fraud?

The rule suggests 10% of people will never commit fraud, 80% might under the right circumstances, and 10% are likely to act regardless. This highlights the need for layered prevention and detection strategies.
