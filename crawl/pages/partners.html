Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know
about Quavo's fintech partnership program.

What is a FinTech partnership?

A FinTech partnership is a collaborative arrangement between a fintech company and another organization to deliver mutual benefits, ranging from referral agreements to deep technology integrations.

What types of partners are you looking for?

Quavo partners with organizations that fit one of five engagement models, each designed to create mutual value while expanding the reach and impact of our fraud and dispute management platform.

  • Issuer Processor Managed Services, typically via OEM or White-Label
  • Embedded Finance / BaaS Platforms
  • Ecosystem / Infrastructure Vendors and Marketplaces Fraud, tokenization, compliance, and workflow-adjacent vendors with integration potential, as well as cloud and enterprise app marketplaces where customers can subscribe to & enable Quavo.
  • Strategic Alliances & Integrations (Co-Sell / Integration) Established fintech and banking technology companies where Quavo builds go-to-market, co-marketing, and deep integration relationships.
  • Data Relationships Two-way dispute intelligence, fraud taxonomy, patterns, and behavioral signals to enrich fraud & dispute models and risk analytics products.
What is an example of a FinTech partnership?

A core banking platform integrating with QFD® to deliver seamless dispute management to shared clients is a classic technology partnership example. Both platforms deliver greater value together, reducing implementation friction for financial institutions.

Who is a good fit for Quavo's partner program?

Whether your goal is revenue growth, market expansion, technology integration, or improving fraud model accuracy, there is a partnership path aligned to your strengths. Ideal companies are growing or best-in-class for their domain and products.

What benefits do partners receive?

Benefits vary by partnership type, but Quavo’s program is built to help partners grow their business, expand visibility, and create more value for their clients.

Issuer Processor Managed Services Partners receive:

  • A white-label or OEM-licensed version of Quavo that reduces dispute operations cost by 40–60% across their FI client base
  • A path to differentiate their managed services offering and win and retain FI clients against competitor processors
  • A revenue-generating add-on to their existing managed services contracts, without a multi-year internal build
  • Quavo’s operational support, implementation expertise, and ongoing product innovation
  • Volume-based pricing tiers that scale with their FI book

Embedded Finance / BaaS Platform Partners receive:

  • A fully integrated dispute management module that extends QFD to their downstream fintech clients without individual sales cycles
  • A new revenue line through revenue sharing on QFD fees adopted by their client base
  • Co-branded go-to-market collateral, joint client rollout sequencing, and dedicated partner support
  • A differentiated platform capability that increases client stickiness and reduces churn

Ecosystem / Infrastructure Vendor and Marketplace Partners receive:

  • A co-sell relationship structured around clear commercial economics
  • Integration support and joint solution development to close the fraud-to-dispute lifecycle gap
  • Co-marketing investment: joint webinars, case studies, conference presence, and partner portal listing

Strategic Alliance & Integration Partners receive:

  • A co-sell or referral relationship with clear economics
  • A joint solution narrative; “detect + resolve” for fraud partners, “comply + execute” for compliance partners
  • Quarterly pipeline reviews and joint QBRs with shared clients
  • Co-marketing investment: joint webinars, case studies, and conference presence

Data Relationships receive:

  • A direct partnership with the only disputes platform with depth of issuer-side resolution data
  • Custom solutions and economics
Do you offer commissions, referral fees, or revenue sharing?

Yes, and the structure is specific to each partnership type. All commercial terms are negotiated as part of the formal partnership agreement.

Quavo does not engage in partnerships that do not yield top-line revenue or strategic leverage. We are not actively engaging in referral-only relationships.
