Frequently Asked Questions

What is recovery automation?

QFD® handles the case creation in Association platforms and executes recovery for disputed transactions made on the Mastercard, Visa, Pulse, and PLUS networks. QFD® will continue to auto-process recovery work by including merchant responses, updating Association cases, and supporting write-off and denial decisions throughout.

How does QFD® support compliance requirements?

QFD® ensures regulatory compliance by automatically executing required actions and updating cases in alignment with SLA mandates and time-sensitive regulations. Built-in timers prioritize manual work where human oversight is essential, ensuring no deadlines are missed. With a suite of configurable compliance settings, QFD delivers automated protection while adapting to your institution’s unique business needs.

What workflows can be automated?

QFD® streamlines dispute management with a comprehensive suite of automated workflows, including card blocking and reissuance for fraud claims, general ledger accounting, temporary authorization handling, accountholder communications, fee matching, and merchant refund application. Automation levels can be finely tuned for specific processes, providing institutions with both AI-driven efficiency and full operational control.

What are QFD®’s data reporting offerings?

QFD® features a built-in reporting browser that provides managers and internal auditors with real-time access to volume and operational data. For deeper insights, Quavo’s Business Intelligence hub delivers advanced reporting with dynamic visualizations, enabling fast and informed decision-making—even when processing large data sets in seconds. 

Through our partnership with Snowflake, we offer a powerful data warehouse packed with analytical and forecasting capabilities, accessible whether or not you have a Snowflake account.

Quavo’s business event streaming service delivers real-time dispute activity directly to your data warehouse, fully customizable to meet your organization’s specific needs.

Need deeper insights? Quavo’s Advanced Data solutions provide access to expanded dashboards and give you the flexibility to create custom reports tailored to your specific needs.

What is chargeback software?
Chargeback software automates the management of payment disputes and chargeback claims for financial institutions. It handles case intake, evidence gathering, compliance timelines, card network filings, and recovery—significantly reducing manual effort while improving outcomes at scale.
