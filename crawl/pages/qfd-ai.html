Still paying the high cost of making it right? Meet Aria™

Meet Aria, the built-in analyst powering Quavo’s AI dispute resolution platform designed to modernize fraud and dispute operations. Within Quavo’s automated dispute resolution software, Aria works alongside your team to streamline investigations, accelerate communication, and scale resolution processes without added burden. Trained on millions of disputes, Aria automates 90% of tasks and executes 100% of actions 24/7—no additional headcount required.

Introducing your team’s newest super analyst, Aria!

Built to modernize fraud and dispute operations, Aria works alongside your team to streamline investigations, accelerate communication workflows, and scale resolution processes without increasing burden. Trained on millions of disputes, Aria can automate 90% of tasks and 100% of actions, coach claims agents, advise account holders, satisfy auditors with a deep audit trail, and ease regulatory concerns with ensured compliance. She integrates with core banking engines, processors, and payment networks. Now it pays to make it right, with Aria. 

Aria supports teams at different levels of automation depending on operational needs. Capture the right level of intelligence and automation that fits your organization through our tiered approach.

Analyze

Aria quietly gathers data that is then analyzed. All data is securely stored and available through our centralized data warehouse. 

Augment

Aria provides investigators with insights drawn from millions of dispute outcomes. She analyzes data, text, and images while enforcing regulatory and network rules for faster, compliant decisions.

Automate

Enable full end-to-end automation, allowing Aria to work disputes completely for a true no-touch experience that accelerates resolution and reduces operational burden.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know
about the AI dispute resolution platform.

What is an AI dispute resolution platform and how does it work?

An AI dispute resolution platform uses machine learning, generative AI, and automated workflow logic to manage payment disputes with minimal human intervention. Aria analyzes transaction data, incoming correspondence, and historical dispute outcomes to make real-time decisions — routing, resolving, or escalating cases based on confidence levels and regulatory requirements.

What are the main benefits of using an AI dispute resolution system?

24/7 processing without staffing constraints, consistent regulatory rule application, elimination of human error, faster resolution timelines, real-time audit trails, reduced operational costs, and the ability to scale dispute volume without adding headcount.

How secure and private are AI dispute resolution platforms?

Aria operates within QFD®, which is SOC 2 Type 2 certified and PCI compliant. All dispute data is processed within secure, encrypted environments with role-based access controls and comprehensive audit logging, meeting FI security requirements at every scale.
